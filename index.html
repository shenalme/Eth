ඇත්කඳ ලිහිණියාATHKANDA LIHINIYA

Forests, rivers, fire and Sri Lankan elephants. A story set deep in the wild.
Written and directed by Sarath Dharmasiri.

The TeledramaInto the wild

Telecast in 2011, Athkanda Lihiniya was shot on location among jungle paths, village huts, rivers and open grasslands, with the elephants and the landscape playing as large a part as the people.

Also searched as Ethkanda Lihiniya, this Sri Lankan teledrama follows the wild landscape of the island, where herds of Sri Lankan elephants bathe in rivers and walk the forest paths.

Fire-lit nights, long chases and quiet moments by the water make up a drama about courage and destiny.
